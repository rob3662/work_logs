How Sub Tracker Works

A simple guide to getting started with Sub Tracker and making the most of your substitute teacher work tracking and EI calculator.

1

Create Your Account

Sign up with your @nlschools.ca or @nlesd.ca email address. After registration, you'll receive a verification email. Once verified, you can log in and start tracking your substitute teaching work.

Tip: You can optionally add a personal email address later for additional login options.

2

Set Up Your Profile

Complete your teaching profile with your certificate level, years worked, and starting sick days for each school year. This information is used to calculate accurate pay estimates based on NLTA schedules.

Tip: Update your profile at the start of each new school year to ensure accurate calculations.

3

Add Your Work Days

Record each substitute teaching assignment with the date, school name, teacher name, hours worked, and other details. You can add work days from the dashboard or calendar view.

Tip: Add work days as soon as possible to keep accurate records and avoid forgetting entries.

4

Track Your Progress

View your dashboard for summary statistics, check your calendar for a visual overview, and use the EI Claim page to track your weekly totals and benefit eligibility.

Tip: Export your data regularly to CSV or PDF for backup and record-keeping.

Key Features Explained

Track every substitute teaching assignment with detailed information:

  • Work Date: The date you worked
  • School & Teacher: Where and for whom you worked
  • Hours Worked: Number of hours (up to 5.00 per day)
  • Pay Rate: Low rate (Schedule B) or High rate (Schedule C) for 3+ consecutive days or Vacant Positions
  • Status: Mark days as paid, sick days, family days, or weather days

Sub Tracker calculates pay estimates using:

  • NLTA Schedule B (low rate) and Schedule C (high rate) data
  • Your certificate level and years worked
  • Hours worked per day
  • For paid/premium users: Detailed deduction breakdowns including EI, CPP, taxes, and union dues

Vacant positions: If you are in a Vacant position (no teacher hired), include the word “vacant” in the teacher name for those jobs. The pay estimator will then use the correct deductions for Teacher Pension Plan (TPP) contributions instead of the substitute pension for those days.

Note: These are estimates. Always verify with your employer's payroll department.

The EI Claim page helps you track eligibility and calculate benefits:

  • Weekly Summaries: View your work days organized by week
  • EI Hours: Hours are scaled to 8-hour days for EI purposes (5 hours worked = 8 EI hours)
  • Income Tracking: Track gross pay per week
  • Benefit Calculator: Select your region—St. John's ({{ "%.1f"|format(ei_nl_rates.st_johns_pct) }}%, {{ ei_nl_rates.st_johns_insured_hours_to_qualify }} h to qualify) or Rest of Province ({{ "%.1f"|format(ei_nl_rates.rest_pct) }}%, {{ ei_nl_rates.rest_insured_hours_to_qualify }} h)—to see estimated benefits
  • Best Weeks: Automatically identifies your best weeks for EI benefit calculation

See our EI Benefits Guide for more detailed information.

Sick Days:

  • Set your starting balance for each school year in your profile
  • Sick days accrue based on hours worked
  • Your balance can go as low as -2.0 days

Family Days:

  • You get 3.0 days per school year
  • Family days do not accrue like sick days
  • Use them wisely throughout the year

Export your data for backup and record-keeping:

  • Work Days Export: CSV or PDF format with filtering options (date range, paid/unpaid status)
  • EI Claim Export: Weekly summaries in CSV or PDF format
  • All exports include comprehensive data for your records

Tip: Export your data regularly to maintain backup records of your employment.
