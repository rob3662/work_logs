Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Robert Brake (“we,” “us,” “our”) collects, uses, and shares personal information in connection with Sub Tracker NL (the “Service”). By using the Service, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account details: email address, username, and optional personal email (if you add one)
  • Work day records (dates, schools, teachers, hours worked, notes, and related fields you choose to enter)
  • School-year and certification-related information you provide (for example, certificate level, years worked)
  • Sick day, family day, and weather day tracking information you enter
  • Billing-related identifiers when you purchase a paid plan (for example, customer identifiers from our payment processor—we do not store full payment card numbers on our servers when payment is handled by a processor)
  • Correspondence when you contact us (feedback, support, registration-related messages)
  • Feature usage data (for example, pages or features used) for product improvement, generally in aggregate where feasible
  • Announcement read status so we do not show duplicate dismissible notices

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, secure, and improve the Service
  • Process and display your work day tracking and related calculations you request
  • Send administrative messages (for example, verification, security, or service updates)
  • Respond to your questions and support requests
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions when billing is enabled
  • Detect, investigate, and help prevent fraud or abuse
  • Analyze usage in aggregate to improve the user experience

3. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data as described below.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access, update, or correct personal information in your account where the Service allows it
  • Request deletion of your account and personal information, subject to legal exceptions
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing is consent-based
  • Request a copy of information you provided, where applicable

To exercise these rights, contact us at {{ PRIVACY_EMAIL }} or through the Contact page. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session, protect against abuse (for example, CSRF protection), and remember preferences such as theme. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling essential cookies may affect how the Service works.

7. Third-Party Services

We use service providers that process information on our behalf, including:

  • Payment processing: For example, Stripe (or other processors we may use) to handle payments and subscription billing. Payment information is subject to the processor’s privacy policy and terms.
  • Hosting and infrastructure: Servers and related services that store or transmit data.
  • Email and operational tools: For transactional email, monitoring, or support, as configured.

These providers access only the information needed to perform their services and are required to protect it appropriately. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page and change the “Last updated” date. For material changes, we may provide additional notice where appropriate.

9. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, email {{ PRIVACY_EMAIL }}. For general support, {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }}. You may also use the Contact page.
