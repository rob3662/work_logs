Frequently Asked Questions

We use NLTA schedule data (Schedule B for low rate, Schedule C for high rate) and the hours you record to estimate pay. The system uses your certificate level, years worked, and the pay rate type (low/high) to calculate daily rates. On the Professional plan, you can view detailed breakdowns including deductions in the Pay Day Information modal.

Regular work hours are scaled to 8-hour days for EI purposes (e.g., 5 hours worked = 8 EI hours). The EI Claim page shows weekly summaries with your insurable hours, income, and helps identify your best weeks for EI benefit calculations. You can select your region—St. John's ({{ "%.1f"|format(ei_nl_rates.st_johns_pct) }}%, {{ ei_nl_rates.st_johns_insured_hours_to_qualify }} h to qualify) or Rest of Province ({{ "%.1f"|format(ei_nl_rates.rest_pct) }}%, {{ ei_nl_rates.rest_insured_hours_to_qualify }} h)—to see estimated benefits; those figures are loaded from Service Canada when the app refreshes.

Registration requires a @nlschools.ca or @nlesd.ca email address. This email must be verified before you can log in for the first time. After your work email is verified, you can optionally add a personal email address in your profile that can also be used for login once verified.

Sick days are tracked per school year. You set your starting sick day balance in your profile for each school year. Sick days accrue based on hours worked, and you can use them when marking a work day as a sick day. Your balance can go as low as -2.0 days. Family days are separate - you get 3.0 days per school year and they don't accrue. Weather days are for tracking days when you were booked but schools were closed due to weather (like snow storms). These tags (sick, family, weather) are mutually exclusive - you can only select one per work day.

Low rate (Schedule B) is the standard pay rate used by default. High rate (Schedule C) applies when you work 3 or more consecutive days for the same teacher or when in a Vacant Position. You can manually select which rate applies to each work day when adding or editing entries. The system calculates daily rates based on your certificate level and years worked.

Yes! You can export your work days to CSV or PDF format with filtering options (date range, paid/unpaid status, etc.). You can also export your EI claim weekly summaries to CSV or PDF. All exports include comprehensive data for your records.

Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Enter your email address and you'll receive a password reset link via email. You can also change your password anytime from your profile page.

Announcements are important messages from administrators displayed at the top of the page. They may include service updates, new features, or important information. Once you dismiss an announcement by clicking the X button, it won't appear again. You can always view past announcements through the admin interface if you have access.

Check the User Guide for detailed instructions. You can also submit feedback to report bugs or request features, or email {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }} for support.

Scheduled maintenance is performed on Saturdays from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM (Newfoundland Time). The site may be temporarily unavailable during this window. We try to keep maintenance windows brief and will post announcements for any extended downtime.
