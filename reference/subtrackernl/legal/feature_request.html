What Makes a Good Feature Request?

When submitting a feature request, please include:

  • Clear Description: What feature would you like to see?
  • Use Case: How would this feature help you in your work?
  • Expected Behavior: How should the feature work?
  • Priority: Is this a nice-to-have or essential feature?

Feature Request Process

Here's what happens with your feature requests:

  1. Your request is logged and reviewed by our team
  2. We evaluate feasibility and user impact
  3. Popular requests may be prioritized for development
  4. You can track the status of your request in "My Feedback"
  5. We may reach out for clarification or additional details
