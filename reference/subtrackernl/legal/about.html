Our Story

Why Sub Tracker exists and what we aim to solve.

Sub Tracker began as a simple way to keep accurate records of substitute teaching days, hours, and pay. It has grown into a focused tool that helps substitutes track work days, estimate pay, and prepare reports while keeping data private and secure.

Our Mission

  • Provide clear, simple tracking of work days and hours
  • Offer practical summaries (pay estimates, EI weeks) per local needs
  • Respect user privacy and security

Privacy & Security

We use HTTPS for all traffic, hashed passwords, rate limiting, and structured logging. See our Privacy Policy for details.
