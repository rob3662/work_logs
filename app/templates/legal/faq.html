Frequently asked questions

Sessions and hours

How do I stop an open session?
On the session page, set end time and/or use Hours (optional override), then Stop. If you enter hours, the stored end time is computed from your start time and those hours.

Where do income and expenses go?
Add income lines and expense lines on the session detail page. The session totals are the sums of those lines; you do not type totals directly on the start/edit session forms.

Can I import Stripe payments?
Yes. Use Import Stripe with a unified payments CSV (gross income + processing fee expenses) or a balance history CSV (Stripe billing/usage stripe_fee rows as expenses; charge rows there are skipped). Files are analyzed in memory and not kept on the server.

Reports

What is the difference between Generic and EI-oriented?
Both use the same underlying sessions. Generic shows full totals including expenses and net. EI-oriented focuses on hours and gross revenue and adds a Sunday-based weekly summary table.

Why does the weekly table sit above the filters on the EI report?
So the weekly snapshot is visible first; change From/To or project below and click Apply to refresh everything together.

What is in the CSV/PDF?
The active tab’s export includes the same filters as the page. EI exports omit expense columns and include a weekly summary block in the CSV; the PDF follows the EI layout.

Account

Who can delete a session?
Only the tenant owner can delete a session (to avoid accidental loss of shared history). Editors can still add lines and stop sessions per your rules.
