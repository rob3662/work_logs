About {{ app_name }}

{{ app_name }} is a lightweight work log for self-employed and independent workers who want a clear history of when they worked, on what, and how much they earned (and optionally spent) on each job or client block.

What it does

  • Sessions — one row per stretch of work: project, date, times, hours, notes.
  • Income and expense lines — optional line items per session so totals stay traceable.
  • Reports — filter by range and project; switch between a generic summary and an EI-oriented layout with weekly rollups; export to CSV or PDF.

What it is not

It is not a full payroll system, accounting package, or government filing tool. Numbers are only as good as what you enter.
