How {{ app_name }} works

{{ app_name }} helps self-employed people log work sessions, attach income and expenses as lines, and run reports for your records (including an EI-oriented view and CSV/PDF exports).

1. Account and workspace

After you register and sign in, your data lives in your workspace (tenant). Other workspaces cannot see your sessions. If you use team invites, only users you add to your tenant can collaborate there.

2. Work sessions

Start a session with project name, work date, and start time. Open sessions can be stopped from the session page: enter an end time, or use Hours (optional override) so hours and end time stay consistent. Income and expenses are entered as lines on the session page; totals on the session are the sums of those lines.

3. Tasks (optional)

You can add short task notes on a session when you need a simple checklist alongside the log.

4. Reports

Open Reports from the navigation. Choose a date range and optional project substring, then Apply.

  • Generic — totals for hours, income, expenses, and net, plus a session list.
  • EI-oriented — emphasizes hours and gross self-employment revenue, with a weekly summary (Sunday-based weeks) for the last four weeks ending on your To date, clipped to your range and filters.

Download CSV or PDF for the active tab; exports use the same filters as the on-screen report.
